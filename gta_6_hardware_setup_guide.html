❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How much SSD storage space will GTA 6 require?

Industry estimates project GTA 6 will require between 150GB to 200GB of available SSD space at launch due to high-resolution textures and dense world detail. Upgrading to a 2TB NVMe M.2 SSD is recommended.

Will GTA 6 support 60 FPS or 120 FPS on consoles?

Rockstar Games has not confirmed target frame rates for base PS5 and Xbox Series X consoles. However, having a TV or monitor with 120Hz and HDMI 2.1 VRR capabilities ensures compatibility with dynamic performance modes.

Why is an NVMe M.2 SSD required for modern consoles?

Standard external hard drives (HDDs) are too slow to stream open-world assets in real time. PS5 and Xbox Series X require PCIe Gen4 NVMe SSDs with speeds of at least 5,500 MB/s to run current-generation titles directly.
